FLAGSHIP PRODUCT · ACERS (ETRM)

ACERS — Energy Trading & Risk Management, front to back.

ACERS is a complete ETRM platform for financial oil and refined-products trading — crude, gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, fuel oil, naphtha and natural gas futures and swaps. Trades flow straight in from ICE through the trade-capture API, prices come from Nasdaq, and initial margins are calculated automatically, so positions, P&L, risk and settlement are always current and fully auditable.

Physical trading, scheduling and inventory can be added as a client-specific build.

ICE trade capture Nasdaq market data Automatic initial margin Futures & swaps
ACERS MODULES

Everything from deal capture to settlement

ICE Trade Capture

Trades booked on ICE flow in automatically through the ICE trade-capture API — no re-keying. Manual entry for OTC swaps, options and spreads.

Position & P&L

Real-time positions by product, book, trader, contract month and strategy; mark-to-market and realised/unrealised P&L.

Market Data (Nasdaq)

Live and end-of-day prices from Nasdaq; forward curves, volatilities and settlement prices for valuation.

Initial Margin

Exchange initial margin per account and portfolio, with margin calls, excess/deficit and what-if margin on new trades.

Risk Management

Value-at-Risk, stress and scenario testing, with exposure consolidated across books, portfolios and counterparties.

Settlement & confirmations

Trade confirmations, daily settlement, variation margin, fees and broker statement reconciliation.

Reporting & dashboards

Configurable reports, dashboards and data exports for traders, risk, finance and management.

Integration

REST APIs and connectors to ICE, Nasdaq, brokers, ERP and treasury systems.

Physical Trading on request

Physical deal capture, scheduling, inventory and quality adjustments, built to your requirements.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built for oil & refined-products trading

Who uses ACERS

  • Oil trading houses and proprietary trading firms
  • Refiners hedging crack spreads and product margins
  • National oil companies and producers hedging output
  • Commodity desks at banks, funds and brokers trading crude and products on ICE

Key benefits

  • One system, one truth. Front, middle and back office work from the same data.
  • Transparent calculations. Every valuation and risk number traces back to its inputs and rules.
  • Configurable, not customised. Commodities, contracts, workflows and reports are set up by configuration, so upgrades stay simple.
  • Fast implementation. Our implementation team takes you from kickoff to go-live.
  • Deploy your way. Cloud, private cloud or on-premise.
