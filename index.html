About Oleovista Solutions

Diverse Global Portfolio, Safe & Responsible Execution

Oleovista Trading manages a diverse portfolio of products and services, safely and responsibly, all over the world. With extensive expertise and knowledge in various fields, we ensure top-quality service and delivery in every market we serve.

Our responsive decision-making approach, combined with our deep understanding of the connection between risk, reward, and sustainable growth, has made us one of the fastest-growing global commodity trading and asset investment companies in the energy sector.

360° Integrated Value Chain
01

Crude Oil

Ensuring seamless and efficient movement of crude oil from producing countries to international refiners and our proprietary refinery network.

02

Marine Fuels

Bunkering operations in Fujairah, UAE—the third largest bunkering market globally—delivering high-grade fuel directly to vessels with green transition protocols.

03

Gasoline

Comprehensive range of gasoline and blending components supported by our Fujairah storage facilities and relationships with international majors.

04

LPG (Liquefied Gas)

Global trade via Very Large Gas Carriers (VLGCs) from the US and Arabian Gulf to Asia, plus pressurized LPG cargoes for regional infrastructure.

05

Gas & Power

Power and gas trading licenses in Turkey and European interconnected grids, enabling active participation in evolving regional energy dynamics.

06

Fuel Oil

Specialized blending operations for marine fuel and utility-grade power generation, producing low-sulfur and high-sulfur compliant fuels.

07

Distillates

Efficient value chain connecting East & Southern Africa with vessel and overland pipeline/truck supply routes exceeding 1M+ metric tons.

08

Naphtha & Low Carbon

Petrochemical cracker feedstocks and emerging renewable biofuels, driving lower carbon intensity across global logistics.

MINERALS & RAW COMMODITIES

METALS & DRY BULK

Transportation, storage, and supply of ferrous, non-ferrous, and industrial minerals from mine to smelter.

Ferro Alloys

Vital alloys for precision steelmaking: ferromolybdenum, ferroniobium, ferrotungsten, ferromanganese, and ferrosilicon upholding rigid metallurgical standards.

Dry Bulk

Petroleum coke, bauxite, alumina, and industrial raw materials handled via chartered bulk carrier vessels connecting major industrial corridors.

Base Metals

Copper, aluminum, nickel, zinc, lead, and tin. Essential conductive and structural elements powering electrification and modern infrastructure.
